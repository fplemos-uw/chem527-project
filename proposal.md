# Project Proposal

## Research Question

How do selected plasma protein abundance ratios change during recombinant human growth hormone treatment and subsequent washout?

## Background and Motivation

Growth hormone (GH) can affect the levels of several proteins in human plasma. Understanding how these proteins change during treatment and after treatment ends may help identify protein patterns associated with GH exposure.

This project will focus on selected proteins reported by Tan et al. (2017), including Afamin, APOL1, ECM1, IGFBP3, ALS, and Lumican.

## Data Source

The data come from the supplementary material of Tan et al. (2017), *Plasma biomarker proteins for detection of human growth hormone administration in athletes*, published in Scientific Reports.

Article: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-09968-7

The Excel file used is `41598_2017_9968_MOESM2_ESM.xls`, specifically the worksheet named `Combined 6 iTRAQ runs`.

Access date: November 8, 2026.

## Data Sufficiency and Limitations

The selected worksheet contains 385 protein entries and 99 columns, including protein identification information and relative abundance ratios from six iTRAQ experiments. The ratios are dimensionless and are reported relative to a common reference sample.

The dataset provides measurements at different time points during GH treatment and washout, so it can be used to describe changes in selected protein abundance ratios over time.

One important limitation is that the iTRAQ samples were pooled from participants. Therefore, the data do not show individual responses or variability between participants. The analysis will focus on descriptive comparisons of the reported protein ratios rather than individual-level statistical conclusions.

Overall, the dataset appears sufficient for a descriptive analysis of selected protein abundance patterns over time, while its limitations will be considered when interpreting the results.
